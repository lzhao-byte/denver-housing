model_xgb_regressor.ubj: tuned XGBRegressor from Notebooks/Part2_Model_Selection_Tuning.ipynb
(n_estimators=134, max_depth=9, learning_rate=0.18, num_parallel_tree=5,
subsample=0.79, colsample_bytree=0.82, gamma=0.17, min_child_weight=2).
